## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a grounding, thoughtful balance between clinical credibility and holistic warmth. Built for modern wellness and mindful nutritional health, the interface balances precision tracking with comforting human reassurance. 

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The visual direction combines **Warm Minimalist Editorial** with **Tactile Soft Surfaces**. Rather than sterile clinical whites or hyper-gamified neon gradients, it leverages deep oceanic slates paired with rich caramel, terracotta undertones, and sun-warmed cream canvases. The aesthetic evokes natural parchment, botanical apothecaries, and bespoke editorial wellness journals.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Health-conscious individuals, intuitive eaters, athletes, and wellness seekers seeking long-term vitality without obsessive guilt or cold metric overload.
- **Emotional Response:** Centered calm, clarity, effortless competence, and nurturing structure. Interactions feel soft, grounding, and dependable.

## Colors

The palette pairs a foundational, high-contrast deep slate navy with warm, roasted earthy accents. Nutritional tracking badges use purposeful natural pigments to ensure rapid macro distinction without visual exhaustion.

### Primary & Accent Roles
- **Primary (`#1B2A3A` - Deep Marine Slate):** Grounds the interface, providing authoritative contrast for primary actions, structural icons, and editorial headlines.
- **Secondary (`#B87A44` - Toasted Caramel):** Highlights mindful achievements, primary active states, and streak milestones with golden warmth.
- **Tertiary (`#4A6B5D` - Herbal Sage):** Balances warm tones with an organic, plant-forward green for positive nutritional targets and vitality cues.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F5F0` - Soft Warm Alabaster):** Replaces harsh pure white with an inviting, parchment-like foundation that reduces ocular fatigue.

### Nutritional & Data Tokens
- **Protein Tag:** Soft Ochre Clay (`#D97736` on tint `#FDF3EC`)
- **Fats Tag:** Olive Sprout (`#5E7343` on tint `#F1F5EB`)
- **Carbohydrates Tag:** Amber Sand (`#C88D27` on tint `#FAF4E8`)
- **Hydration / Micronutrients:** Clear Glacial Sky (`#3D708F` on tint `#EDF4F8`)

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on the geometric warmth and open apertures of Plus Jakarta Sans. Its rounded structural geometry delivers contemporary legibility while preserving an approachable, non-intimidating character.

### Typographic Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Display & Headlines:** Use tighter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and medium-to-bold weights to convey a confident, curated editorial aesthetic.
- **Numerical Nutrients & Metrics:** The dedicated `metric-numeral` level pairs tabular alignment with bold weights, ensuring calorie, gram, and macro tallies scan effortlessly within meal cards.
- **Labels & Tags:** Apply uppercase transforms sparingly and only with `label-sm` to maintain lightness and avoid visual clutter across dense food logs.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first 4-column layout fluidly expands to an 8-column layout on tablets and 12 columns on desktop screens.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Base Grid Unit:** An intentional 4px base with an 8px standard step establishes predictable visual breathing room.
- **Mobile Hand Zone:** Crucial interactive triggers (macro loggers, meal scanning, daily completion toggles) anchor within the lower 40% of the screen with a standard `1.25rem` screen margin.
- **Content Density:** Daily logging feeds prioritize vertical compression using `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps, while summary dashboards and hero insights expand into `space-lg` and `space-xl` containers for mental decompression.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids stark floating drop shadows, instead using **tonal nesting** and **delicate warm-tinted ambient glows** that mimic paper and stone surfaces under natural morning sunlight.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Pure `#F8F5F0` backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces delineated by a 1px soft hairline border of `#EAE3D9` rather than sharp drop shadows.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Modals):** White background layered over a dual-glow ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 42, 58, 0.05), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(184, 122, 68, 0.07);`
- **Level 3 (Toasts & Overlays):** Deep navy `#1B2A3A` surfaces with diffused warm slate blur, focusing urgent nutrition prompts or logging confirmations.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, balanced corner strategy (Level 2). This provides smooth, organic containment without slipping into child-like or purely spherical geometries.

### Shape Expression
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Standardized to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for an ergonomic, card-deck tactile impression.
- **Macro Badges & Nutritional Tags:** Nested pills using full border radiuses to isolate discrete nutritional data effortlessly.
- **Buttons & Search Inputs:** Governed by `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to retain structural authority for actionable elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Slate Navy (`#1B2A3A`) fill with crisp off-white (`#F8F5F0`) text. Active state subtly scales to 98% with an earthen caramel border glow.
- **Secondary:** Warm Alabaster (`#FFFFFF`) surface framed with 1.5px `#E2D9CD` border, text set in `#1B2A3A`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with `#B87A44` caramel text and a delicate `#F3ECE2` fill on hover/press.

### Macro & Nutrient Tracking Tags
- Compact, high-readability chips containing the macro initial, weight, and color-coded indicator dot.
- Built using high-contrast, low-saturation pastel backings with deeply saturated foreground text (e.g., Protein: `#D97736` text over `#FDF3EC` surface).

### Cards
- Set on crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces embedded over `#F8F5F0`.
- Bordered with an ultrathin 1px `#ECE5DB` stroke.
- Inner card padding standardizes to `space-md` (16px) for mobile feeds and `space-lg` (24px) for desktop analytics.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background set to `#FFFFFF` with an inset 1px `#DED5C7` border.
- Floating labels in `#7A756C` transition into `#1B2A3A` on focus.
- Focus ring: 2px soft caramel glow (`rgba(184, 122, 68, 0.3)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radios feature custom nested bullseyes in deep navy.
- Habit-completion checkboxes feature a rounded-sm box with a smooth fill transition into `#4A6B5D` (Sage) displaying a white botanical tick upon goal completion.

### Nutritional Ring & Progress Trackers
- Circular and linear progress meters use soft, unmetered clay paths (`#EFE8DE`) as tracking tracks, filled with rounded, gradient-free solid blocks of Navy, Caramel, and Sage for unequivocal data comprehension.
